How it works

You approve the person. We do everything else.

No job adverts to write, no shortlists to sift, no contracts to draft. Here is exactly what happens between your first call and your assistant’s first week.

Book a free consultation
The process

Five steps, about five working days

Most of the work happens on our side. Your total time commitment before day one is usually under two hours.

What we’ll ask you for

  • A rough list of what eats your week
  • The tools your business runs on
  • The hours of day you actually need covered
  • One hour for the intro call and kick-off
1

Discovery call — day one

Thirty minutes with a matcher, not a salesperson. We run a short delegation audit: what fills your week, what is repeatable, and what a realistic first month looks like. You leave with a plan whether or not you go ahead.

2

Role scoping — day one to two

We turn that audit into a role brief: responsibilities, tools, hours, time-zone overlap and the outcomes you want by week four. You approve the brief before we go looking.

3

Matching — day two to four

We shortlist from the Hive on skills, sector experience and availability. You get one or two profiles with a short intro video, not a stack of CVs to wade through.

4

Meet your match — day four

A 30-minute introduction call. You are meeting a person, not interviewing a candidate — but if the chemistry is not there, say so and we go again at no cost.

5

Onboarding — day five

Your client manager sets up tool access, the shared task board and a first-week plan. Contracts, NDAs and the data processing agreement are handled in the background.

Week one

The first five days, in practice

A good first week is mostly about capturing how you already do things — so it never has to be explained twice.

Shadow & capture

Your assistant observes the recurring work and writes it down as they go. By Friday you have documented processes you did not have on Monday.

Take the first tasks

We start with low-risk, high-volume work — inbox triage, scheduling, data entry — so you see output fast without betting anything important on week one.

Daily check-in

A short written update at the end of each day: done, in progress, blocked. Five minutes to read, no meeting required.

Friday review

Your client manager reviews the week with you and agrees what moves across next. Most clients double the scope by week three.

Who does what

The bits people assume they’ll be stuck with

Hiring someone yourself means owning all of this. With TaskHive, most of it is simply not your problem.

You own

  • Setting priorities and approving the work
  • Deciding what moves across and when
  • Granting access to your systems
  • Telling us when something isn’t working

Typically under an hour a week after the first fortnight.

We own

  • Sourcing, vetting, referencing and screening
  • Contracts, payroll, NI, pension and equipment
  • Day-to-day management and quality review
  • Holiday cover, sickness cover and rematching
  • Training, development and retention
Trust & security

Handing over access, safely

Delegation only works if you can stop worrying about it. These are the controls that come as standard.

Screened before placement

Identity and right-to-work checks, two verified references and background screening appropriate to the role — all completed before anyone is introduced to you.

Your access, your control

We work inside your systems using your own permissions and password manager, with least-privilege access. Revoke it yourself, instantly, at any time.

Contracted properly

Signed NDAs, UK GDPR compliance and a data processing agreement with every client. Confidentiality obligations sit with TaskHive, not just the individual.

Start with a conversation, not a contract

Thirty minutes to map what you could hand over. No obligation, and you keep the plan either way.
