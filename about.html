About TaskHive

Good support shouldn’t depend on headcount

We built TaskHive because capable businesses were being held back by work that did not need a permanent hire — and capable people were being held back by jobs that did not need to be in an office.

Our story

Two problems that turned out to be one

Small and mid-sized businesses kept telling us the same thing: they needed about a day and a half of proper support a week. Not a full-time salary, not a freelancer who disappears mid-project — something in between, that nobody was selling.

At the same time, we knew brilliant assistants, coordinators and administrators who had left the workforce or gone under-employed because the only roles on offer were full-time, on-site, and inflexible.

TaskHive exists to close that gap. We build long-term relationships between businesses that need flexible support and professionals who want meaningful, properly managed remote work — and we take responsibility for making the match work, not just making it.

What we believe

Four things we won’t compromise on

People, not resources

Our professionals are paid fairly, trained continuously and given a career path — because the alternative is churn, and churn is your problem before it is ours. We would rather grow slowly than staff badly.

Honest about hours

Time is tracked against the task it was spent on and reported weekly. If you are paying for sixty hours and using forty, we will tell you to move down a tier rather than quietly bank the difference.

Your processes stay yours

Everything your assistant learns gets documented in your workspace, not ours. If you ever leave, you leave with a written operating manual you did not have before.

Earn the access

We are asking for your inbox, your CRM and sometimes your bank reconciliation. Screening, NDAs, least-privilege access and UK GDPR compliance are the floor, not a premium add-on.

The Hive

How we build the team you’ll meet

“The Hive” is our network of vetted professionals. Getting in is deliberately hard, because the alternative is passing the filtering job to you.

Apply

Open applications from the UK and internationally. We look for relevant experience, written English and evidence of working independently.

Assess

A practical, role-specific task — a real inbox to triage, a real spreadsheet to clean — marked against a rubric, not a gut feeling.

Interview & verify

A live interview with a TaskHive manager, two verified professional references and background screening appropriate to the role.

Onboard & develop

Data protection and tooling training before placement, then ongoing development and quality reviews for as long as they are with us.
