How it works

From first call to first task in under a week

No job adverts, no interviews to run, no contracts to draft. We handle the matching, you approve the person.

Tell us what you need

A 30-minute discovery call. We map the tasks eating your week, the tools you use and the hours that would actually help.

We find your match

We shortlist from the Hive on skills, sector experience and time-zone overlap — then send you a profile with a short intro video.

Meet and onboard

You meet them before you commit. Your client manager sets up tools, access and a first-week plan so week one is productive, not awkward.

Scale up or down

Change hours month to month, add a specialist, or pause. Holiday and sickness cover is arranged by us, not left to you.

The things people ask before they book

We work with a UK-based team and a wider international network, so we can match you on skills, sector and time-zone overlap rather than geography alone. If you need someone working entirely within UK hours, or someone covering evenings and weekends, tell us on the discovery call and we will shortlist accordingly.

Most clients go from first call to first task inside five working days. Straightforward admin and customer service roles can move faster; specialist or multilingual requirements occasionally take a little longer. We will give you a realistic date on the call rather than an optimistic one.

Tell your client manager and we will rematch you at no extra cost. Because the processes your assistant documents live in your workspace rather than in their head, a handover is a handover — not a restart. In practice most wobbles are a briefing problem we can fix in a week.

Every professional signs an NDA and completes data protection training before they are placed. We operate under UK GDPR, sign a data processing agreement with every client, and use your own access controls and password manager wherever possible so you can revoke access instantly.

Yes. Plans are rolling monthly with 30 days’ notice. Move up or down a tier, add a second specialist for a busy quarter, or pause if your business is seasonal. There are no recruitment fees and no exit penalties.

You set the priorities; we handle the management. From the Growth plan upwards you get a named client manager who runs quality checks, handles absence cover, and sends you a weekly summary. Most clients spend under an hour a week on oversight after the first fortnight.
