Not sure where to start?

Most people delegate the wrong things first

The instinct is to hand over whatever you dislike most. The better test is whether a task is repeatable, documentable and low-judgement — those three go first, and they are usually worth more hours than you expect.

On your consultation we run a short delegation audit: we list what fills your week, sort it against those three tests, and show you what the first month could realistically take off your plate.
