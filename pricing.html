Pricing questions

Before you commit

Hours are pooled across the month rather than allocated daily, so a quiet fortnight followed by a busy one is fine. Starter plans roll over up to four unused hours into the next month; Growth and Dedicated roll over up to 10%. Beyond that, unused hours expire — if you are consistently under, we will suggest you move down a tier rather than let you overpay.

Additional hours are charged at your plan’s hourly rate, and we will flag it before you get there rather than after. If it happens twice running, moving up a tier is cheaper — we will tell you that too.

No. Matching, onboarding, contracts and tool setup are all included in the monthly fee. There is no fee to start and no fee to leave.

On the Dedicated plan, yes — up to three specialists, which is how most clients build a small support pod covering admin, customer service and finance. On Starter and Growth you get one dedicated professional, because splitting a small number of hours tends to cost more in context-switching than it saves.

Monthly in advance by direct debit or card, with an itemised invoice and a time report showing where the hours went. Annual plans are invoiced up front at a 10% discount. All prices exclude VAT.

Yes. We invoice international clients in EUR or USD at a rate fixed for the length of your plan, so your monthly cost does not move with the exchange rate.
